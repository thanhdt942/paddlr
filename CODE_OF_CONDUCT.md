# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As contributors, maintainers, and community members of the HolyShitItWorks project, we pledge to foster an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy environment for everyone.

We commit to participating without harassment, discrimination, or intimidation, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Standards

We strive to build a community that values:
- **Constructive Collaboration:** Focusing on what is best for the overall engineering discipline and community.
- **Thoughtful Feedback:** Offering and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback.
- **Empathy and Respect:** Treating all community members, users, and contributors with dignity and consideration.
- **Evidence-Based Discussions:** Grounding technical discussions, skill reviews, and architecture debates in evidence, clarity, and rational trade-offs.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behaviors include:
- Harassing, derogatory, or discriminatory language, comments, and personal attacks.
- Public or private harassment in any form.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Disruptive conduct in project repositories, issue trackers, discussions, or social channels.
- Any other behavior reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional open-source environment.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate.

To report violations of this Code of Conduct:
- Contact the project maintainers via the issue tracker or project communication channels designated for moderation.
- All reports will be reviewed promptly and investigated fairly, maintaining confidentiality regarding the reporter.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by project leadership.
